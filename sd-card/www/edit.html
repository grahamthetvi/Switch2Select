Setup

This page saves onto the card in the device and the student screen uses it right away. To leave that screen up, join Switch2Select on a phone and open http://192.168.4.1/www/edit.html there.

Scanning

The four switches always choose that choice. While choices move on their own, the scan switch chooses the lit one. While the scan switch steps, it only moves ahead.

Volume and card lights

Zero leaves the lights off. The plus and minus buttons on the box also change the volume.

The four choices

The scanning word is spoken as each choice lights up. A picture, video, or game says that word when it is chosen. Words on the screen say the sentence, or the scanning word when the sentence is blank. With the screen closed, the speech box says the sentence.
